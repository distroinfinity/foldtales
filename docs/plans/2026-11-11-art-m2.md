## Design

**The gap sits on the book's spine.** Pip is on the right page and the lighthouse is on the left, so crossing is crossing the book. Each layer already splits at the spine, so nothing has to fold through the chasm.

**The gap is sized from the objects.** The ladder is 150 mm and the sock is 90 mm. The sock is written as far too short, and the ladder has to reach. The chasm on the printed ground, at Pip's depth (z = -0.0175 m), must measure **92 to 128 mm** wide, centered on the spine within 12 mm. The target is one quarter of the width, 105 mm. The floor is wider than the sock. The ceiling leaves 10 mm or more of ladder resting on each side, using the ladder's painted length (about 148.3 mm, measured from its PNG, because its 150 mm `heightM` includes transparent padding).

Rims are measured from the finished ground, and the marks and `gapM` are written from that measurement rather than assumed. The make script writes the ladder's measured span into `gapM` beside them, so the vitest test can check it without decoding a PNG.

**Stage layout**, same space as `shore-path/set.json`:

| Layer | File | Hinge depth from near edge | Height limit | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `../shore-path/layer-1-sky.png` | 295 mm | as is | Reused |
| 2 | `../shore-path/layer-2-headland.png` | 250 mm | as is | Reused, with the lighthouse |
| 3 | `layer-3-far-rim.png` | 215 mm | 120 mm | Two bluffs. Stops at shore's dune height so the lighthouse stays visible |
| Pip | | 175 mm | | Marks below |
| 4 | `layer-4-near-rim.png` | 115 mm | 100 mm | Two bluffs, low near the gap so Pip's legs read |

Each bluff is its own card, so each is **shifted to register against the chasm printed at that layer's own hinge depth**, its inner edge 4 mm back from the printed edge. The shift is free and is limited to 5% of the width per bluff. After registering, every inner edge must sit from 6 mm over the chasm to 12 mm back from it.

Layers 3 and 4 are each **two separate pieces** on one painting, with nothing across the middle (no paint within 35 mm of the spine). They're placed on the shared 2720 px canvas, so `splitColumnPx` and the card logic stay the same as shore-path's.

**Marks** (written by the make script from the measured rims): `near-edge` (Pip, measured right rim + 25 mm), `far-side` (left rim − 25 mm), `gap-middle` (spine, on Pip's depth), `wick-arrives` (x = -0.13), `lighthouse-way` (x = -0.19), `moth-hover` (over the gap, y = 0.09).

**Drawer.** It comes out of the page block, under the pull, which sits at x = 0.105 m (`art/m1-proof.html`). Tray outer 190 × 125 mm (interior 176 × 111), front 190 × 26 mm, rail 125 × 26 mm, all at Pip's density (5.32 px/mm), so objects sit on it at one scale. `drawer.json` gives sizes, `travelM` 0.12, and a slot for each of the five objects. Open, they stand upright as cards, because laid flat they'd need 25,500 mm² against a 19,500 mm² floor.

Cards up to 148 mm tall can't stand inside a 30 mm book, and folding them back or forward won't work either: the ladder is longer than the tray is deep, and a backward sweep passes through the pages. So **the stow runs across the tray, in three stages, with the tray fully out before anything moves:**

1. **Stowed:** the object lies flat on the felt, its long axis across the tray (the ladder is 148 mm against a 176 mm interior). The five overlap like things in a real drawer, and they're paper-thin, so a stack fits under the 26 mm walls.
2. **Tip up** on its long edge, about a hinge along x on its own plane, lying behind that line while flat (the way the set's cards stand up), so it stands on its side.
3. **Swing upright** in its own vertical plane, like standing up a ladder, sliding from its flat position to its standing position with its lowest point kept on the felt.

Each slot in `drawer.json` gives a `flatXM`, a `standXM`, a `planeZM` and a `turn` (which way it swings, so its low end moves inward, away from the wall). Every object has its own plane, so a swing passes in front of a neighbour instead of through it. Fully open, the interior starts 2 mm past the book's edge, so nothing sweeps through the pages. Closing reverses it. Checked numerically, per slot and at 5° steps: the flat footprint and the standing base stay inside the interior, no corner goes below the felt, no card edge passes through a wall below its 26 mm top, the planes are distinct, and the interior clears the book edge at full travel. The proof page plays the whole open and close.

**Satchel.** Beside the book, right of the fore-edge. Body 130 × 105 mm. `satchel.json` gives the three parts, the flap's hinge (top edge of the back) and a suggested position. Brown leather and a brass buckle, so the gold tab that opens it has an echo.

**Rope.** `rope-coil` is 70 mm high (a round coil, so it clears the 64 mm grab floor on its longest side) with tags `rope`, `ties`, and sits with Wick, not in the drawer. The two spans are 130 mm long and are stretched by the stage to fit: rope 6 mm thick, thread 2 mm.
