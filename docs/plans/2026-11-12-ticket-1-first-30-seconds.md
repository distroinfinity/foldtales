# Ticket 1: First 30 seconds (ends at M1)

**Status:** Done. M1 signed off by the owner on the Quest 3 on Nov 13, after headset session 2. Runs 008 and 009 are closed, and the whole-branch review's Critical and Important findings are fixed. Progress ledger: `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-12-ticket-1-first-30-seconds/progress.md`.

**Goal.** You tap "Open the book" and the theater finds your table and shows it found it. Your room dims, the book unfolds, a gust blows Pip's lantern out, and Pip looks up at *you*. Any frame of it could go in the video. Behind that moment: the whole Painted Stage Kit and Stage and room workstreams, so it holds up on a tray or a desk, with or without passthrough, through a pause, a headset-off and a crash.

**Read with:** `docs/sprint-plan.md` (ticket 1), `docs/technical-design.md` (Stage and your room, Puppet system), `docs/design-language.md` (the look bar), `docs/playtest-notes.md` (open items), `art/m1-proof.html` (the approved timing), `art/cast/prototype.html` (the approved acting).

## The moment, beat by beat

Times run from the moment the book settles. The unfold reuses `m1-proof.html`'s approved timing.

| When | What you see | What you hear |
|---|---|---|
| Landing | The closed book, "Open the book", and the cloud-voice note and mic request in plain words. Under it: "Sit at a table, and look down at it once you're in." | — |
| Searching | A warm ember drifts about 0.6 m in front of you and sinks toward table height, leading your eyes down. No text in the air. Every XR shader variant warms up out of sight meanwhile | A faint warm hum |
| Found | The ember lands. A painted wash blooms across your real table with a gold-edged rim, and "Your theater will play here" printed in it, at 1° or more after scaling | A soft paper bloom |
| 0 s | The closed book drops the last centimeter onto the wash, with its contact shadow | Book thump on wood |
| 0 to 2 s | House lights go down: about 68% dim at the room's edges, with a warm pool around the book. The wash fades to a faint stain under the book | Room tone fades in |
| 0.6 to 2.4 s | The cover swings open about the spine. The title is set in Fraunces on the cover | Cloth and board, then a page settling |
| 2.5 to 4 s | Layers rise back to front with a slight overshoot. Pip rises as a card in the stack. Shadows grow as each layer stands | One paper fold per layer |
| about 4 s | The narrator's line, with a caption printed on the page's caption band. The lantern glows, and its pool of light warms the ground and dunes around Pip | Narrator |
| 5.6 s | The gust: leaves cross the spread, the foreground reeds shiver, the lantern swings hard | Wind gust |
| 6.4 s | The flame dies. The lantern swaps to dark, a curl of smoke rises, the glow and the pool go out, and the mood tints from dusk toward night | Soft puff, brass creak |
| 6.5 s | Pip is frightened: the scared head, a shake, the tail tucked | A small gasp |
| 7.6 s | **Pip looks up at you.** The up head, a chin lift, and his card yaws toward your real head | Silence. It counts as polish |
| After | Pip waits with the dark lantern and glances up at you every few seconds, following you as you lean. M2 starts here | Room tone |

The narrator's line is timed to end before the gust. If its audio runs long or fails, the caption shows for its estimated duration and the gust still lands on time. Nothing is allowed to cover the final silence.

## How it's built

Engine code knows no character names, and everything named is data (the repo test enforces this).

| Unit | Does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Show manifest** | Client data that the contracts leave to us: each cast member's `CastMember` plus its visual states (lantern lit or out), effect anchors (glow, smoke, pool), the book and set metadata, and each cue's implementation (gust, lantern out, open the book, beam). The contracts stay unchanged, and this boundary is explicit | `client/public/show/` |
| **Card material** | One shared material with two complete configurations. **Blend** (the default, approved at M0): premultiplied texture, `One / OneMinusSrcAlpha`, alpha test 0.25, no depth write on soft parts, sorted by rig order. **Coverage:** straight alpha, `alphaToCoverage` with depth write, no blending. Both have a mood tint uniform and the lantern's pool of light as a uniform. Any occlusion factor scales RGB and alpha together, so a premultiplied edge can't fringe. The two are compared on the Quest's real XR framebuffer | `client/src/paint/` |
| **Puppet** | Builds any rig from `rig.json` plus the manifest: mirrored parts, flipped characters, head swaps by visibility, the chin shadow, a contact shadow, held-part variants, effects attached to the animated held part, and card yaw toward you with a limit, at 12 poses a second | `client/src/puppet/` |
| **Acting** | Every action in `ACTING` as a pure pose function over the anatomy, plus talking heads, all driven by the show clock | `client/src/puppet/acting/` |
| **Book and sets** | The book from `book.json`, pop-up layers from `set.json`: split halves on hinges, page-colored backs, rise and close order, overshoot, card thickness at the edges, ribbon, tabs and drawer pull. The title and captions are set with `troika-three-text` on the book's surfaces | `client/src/book/` |
| **Room light** | The dim sphere: drawn first, no depth write and no depth test, so the stage always draws over it. It covers passthrough only. Also the warm pool around the book, the mood tint, the lantern glow, and the beam sector cut from the same sphere (P5). In the painted room, the same values tint the room's own materials | `client/src/light/` |
| **Sound** | Web Audio through the existing chain: one-shots positioned on the book, room tone, the narrator line, and ducking. Audio is unlocked by the landing tap. One-shots are scheduled from the show clock, so pausing suspends them and resuming never replays a sound that already played | `client/src/sound/` |
| **Show** | A runner that plays authored `Beat`s by the contract's timing rules (groups, `withPrev`, nominal durations, preemption) on **one pause-aware show clock**. Acting, cues, light and audio all read that clock, and the SDK's elapsed time never drives the show. Every event goes to the IndexedDB log | `client/src/show/` |
| **Checkpoint** | A versioned local checkpoint, written on each event and every second: beat cursor, show clock, book state, lighting, held-part states, completed cues and the stage's scale. Restoring rebuilds state deterministically from it, never replays narration or foley, and re-places the stage in the new XR space. It's client-only, so it isn't a shared schema | `client/src/show/` |
| **Pause** | One state machine with independent reasons (book closed, ribbon, headset off, hands lost). Each trigger is idempotent, the show resumes only once every reason has cleared, and book controls keep animating while the show is frozen. **Hands lost** pauses only during a grab, so a viewer resting their hands out of view doesn't stop the story | `client/src/show/` |
| **Interaction** | Pinch detection from tracked hand joints, with proxies on the cover's edge, the spine and the ribbon. Each target is at least 64 mm, and one gesture owns a hand at a time. The cover is hinge-constrained: past halfway on release it closes, otherwise it springs back. The spine slides on the surface plane, and the book turns to face you on release. Losing tracking mid-gesture cancels it back to the last stable state. Each state has a visual and a sound: near is a soft gold edge, pinch a dip and a card tap, holding a lift and a rustle | `client/src/stage/` |
| **Stage** | Placement from M0, plus provisional versus confirmed surfaces, sizing, the found-surface moment, crash recovery, the frame guard and preloading | `client/src/stage/` |
| **Landing** | The 2D first frame. XR is offered only from the button (`offer: "none"`, with entry from the same click). Mic flow: ask on the landing page, then close the permission stream at once. Granted, denied and ignored all lead to the show, because speech is optional. Preloading runs while you read, and the button waits for it | `client/index.html`, `client/src/landing/` |
| **Asset build** | Per-character and per-set atlases with gutters. Each part keeps its original pixel size and a UV rectangle in a manifest. Encoded to KTX2 (`ktx2-encoder`), with straight or premultiplied alpha matched to the material configuration, and mipmaps baked in. At runtime: `KTX2Loader` with the Basis transcoder served locally, support detection, and PNG as the fallback. A parity check compares PNG and KTX2 renders, and a build check makes sure every referenced file is emitted | `scripts/build-art.ts`, `client/src/paint/` |

**Placement and sizing.**
- **Provisional and confirmed surfaces.** Only a confirmed surface (a held hit-test cluster, or a scanned plane) gets the wash with "Your theater will play here". If the 5 s search ends with nothing, the book appears provisionally in front of you on a quieter wash with no words, and the ember stays near it as a hint.
- **Before the unfold starts,** a confirmed surface may still take over, with a visible lift and settle, never a jump.
- **Once the unfold starts, the stage never moves by itself.** Only you move it, by the spine.
- **Sizing uses scanned planes when the headset has them.** Their polygon gives a real extent, and the scale is chosen so the whole open footprint, the wash and the controls fit and stay within 0.6 m reach.
- **Without planes,** the book opens at desk size if the open footprint is within reach, and steps down toward 0.7 if not. If even the minimum doesn't fit, it opens at the minimum and you can slide it by the spine.
- **After sizing,** text is checked to stay at 1° or more at its real distance.

**Preloading.**
- **Before XR:** fetch, decode and upload every texture, including hidden heads, the dark lantern, effects and the fonts.
- **During the search:** render every object once out of sight, so the XR, multiview and occlusion shader variants compile before the book appears.
- **Measured:** cold start, plus telemetry hitches at the first use of each effect.

**Frame guard.** IWSDK already catches errors in `onXRFrame` callbacks, but only logs them. Ours wraps the show's frame work, reports each error to telemetry (rate-limited) and keeps going. The test proves that the callbacks after a failing one still run.

## Risks

| Risk | If it happens |
|---|---|
| The puppets read as flat from the side (P0) | A lower yaw limit, a thicker card edge, a small second layer behind the coat |
| The dim or beam doesn't sell on passthrough (P5) | A soft overlay near the book only, and a beam in the air |
| Coverage doesn't resolve on multiview | Keep the blend configuration, the M0 default |
| KTX2 softens the line work | UASTC for heads and faces, a higher quality setting for cast atlases |
| Hand pinch on the cover is fiddly | Bigger proxies, the ribbon as the easy pause, then ticket 2's hand work |
| Run 008 or 009 misses | Repaint once inside the cap. Otherwise the room falls back to free art, and sound to CC0 clips |
| No surface and no planes | The provisional placement keeps the show going, and the spine moves it |

## Not in this ticket

Hand-over and the drawer (ticket 2), the scheduler beyond authored beats, the director, voices beyond one line, and Talk. The beam's sweep plays at the end of M2. Here it only gets built and reviewed.
