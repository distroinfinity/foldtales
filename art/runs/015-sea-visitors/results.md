# Run 015: sea and visitors, results

**Closed Nov 15, 2026.** Spent **23 credits** of the 35 cap: the sea sheet (9), the visitors sheet (9) and the water wash (5). Within the 180 the owner approved for the room effects' art and sound (46 used so far, with run 014).

**The shared balance fell 268 during the first invocation,** and the tool, which measures spend from the balance, logged the visitors sheet at 259 credits and stopped at its cap before the water wash. A 2K sheet costs 9 (every one before it, and the sea sheet in this same run), so the other **250 were another session spending at the same moment**. The manifest keeps the balance drop and the real cost side by side. The balance was steady for a minute before the run started, so a check before isn't enough: someone can start spending in the middle.

## What we learned

- **The shared balance can't measure one run while another session spends.** Reconcile each job against its known price (9 for 2K, 5 for 1K) and record both numbers.
- **Run 014's lessons held:** the full-bleed water wash came out clean (no magenta), and asking for solid painted pieces (waves, foam, boat, bottle, gull) avoided the teardrop and lilac problems.
- **The visitors kept one scale on one sheet** when told so: the boat, bottle and gull read at believable sizes to each other.
- **Still to judge in the headset:** the sea, the boat, the bottle and the gulls, once built.
